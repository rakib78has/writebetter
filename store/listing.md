## Store listing tab

**Name:** WriteBetter

**Summary** (from the manifest, 78 of 132 characters):
Fix grammar and spelling in text boxes and editors, privately, on your device.

**Category:** Tools (alternative: Productivity)

**Language:** English

**Description:**

> Fix grammar and spelling without copying your text anywhere.
>
> Select text in a text box or a rich-text editor, open WriteBetter from its toolbar icon, the right-click menu or Alt+Shift+W, and click Fix grammar. You see every change, removed and added words marked, before anything happens. Then copy the corrected text, or put it back on the page after you confirm.
>
> PRIVATE BY DESIGN
> • Grammar checking runs entirely on your device. Your text is never sent to a server, and there is no account.
> • WriteBetter reads only the text you selected, and only when you ask. It never reads password, verification-code or payment fields.
> • Your text is never stored. Close the popup and it is gone. The only thing remembered is your choice of English variant.
>
> POLISH STYLE
> Optional style suggestions, such as "very unique" or spelling out "ASAP". Each one is a choice you tick; nothing is applied on its own.
>
> CAREFUL WITH YOUR WORDS
> • Names, web addresses, email addresses, code and file names are never "corrected".
> • Anything WriteBetter isn't sure about is listed for you to decide, not changed.
> • Replacing text needs your confirmation, keeps bold text and links, and can be undone with Ctrl+Z.
>
> WORKS WITH
> Ordinary text boxes and rich-text editors, including Gmail-style editors and editors built on ProseMirror, Quill, Lexical, Slate and TinyMCE.
>
> GOOD TO KNOW
> • English only: American, British, Australian, Canadian or Indian spelling, picked from your browser language and changeable in the popup.
> • Grammar checking is rule-based (powered by the open-source Harper engine). It catches common mistakes but can miss some, so give the result a read.
> • Editors inside embedded frames (such as classic TinyMCE) and Google Docs are not supported; copy and paste works there.

## Privacy practices tab

**Single purpose:**
WriteBetter corrects grammar and spelling in text the user selects on a web page, and can put the corrected text back on the page when the user confirms.

**Permission justifications:**

| Permission     | Justification                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `activeTab`    | To read the text the user has selected on the current tab, only after the user clicks the toolbar icon, chooses the context-menu entry or presses the shortcut.       |
| `scripting`    | To run WriteBetter's selection reader in that tab at that moment, and to write the corrected text back after the user confirms. No script runs on any page otherwise. |
| `contextMenus` | To offer "Improve with WriteBetter" in the right-click menu of text fields.                                                                                           |
| `storage`      | To remember the user's chosen English variant (American, British, Australian, Canadian or Indian) on this device. No text is stored.                                  |

**Host permissions:** none.

**Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code. All code, including the WebAssembly grammar engine, is in the package.

**Data usage:** WriteBetter does not collect or transmit any user data. Selected text is processed in the browser and discarded when the popup closes. In the data-type checklist, tick none. Certify:

- I do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, outside of the approved use cases.
- I do not use or transfer user data for purposes that are unrelated to my item's single purpose.
- I do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

**Privacy policy URL:** host [docs/privacy.md](../docs/privacy.md) publicly (for example as the GitHub-rendered file once the repository is public, or on a project page) and link it here.
